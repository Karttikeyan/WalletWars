Every Wallet Tells a Story

Your On-Chain History Is Your Combat Card

Connect your wallet and WalletWars reads your real Base activity — no mock stats, no pay-to-win. What you've actually done on-chain becomes the weapon you fight with.

🔗
Step 1

Connect

Sign in with your wallet — no gas, no transaction. Just a signature to prove it's really yours.

🔨
Step 2

Forge

Your transactions, age on Base, contracts touched, tokens held, and gas burned become five real combat stats.

⚔
Step 3

Battle

Duel other wallets best-of-5, round by round, stat by stat. Win, climb the board, evolve your card.

Beyond Raw Stats

Strategy Wins Duels, Not Just Power

A best-of-5 duel isn't decided by Wallet Power alone. You choose the order your cards fight in, hide your strongest for last — or first, to break morale — and arm yourself with wildcards earned through real on-chain behavior.

🃏
Wildcards

Earned, Not Bought

Veteran's Gambit, Multichain Strike, Builder's Override, Giant Killer — each wildcard unlocks only if your wallet's history actually earns it. No credential, no wildcard.

🎖
Badges

Trophies From Battle

Win streaks, giant-slaying upsets, season milestones — badges attach to your card permanently, visible to every wallet you face in the arena.

🧠
Battle Plan

Order Is a Weapon

Set your five-card order and wildcard before the duel starts. Your opponent only sees rank and shape — never your plan. Outsmart, don't just outgun.
